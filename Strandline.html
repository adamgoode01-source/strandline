Acceptance criteria

Verify before this sheet goes to the field. The tolerances above are whatever you type here — the app does not assert them as code. Elongation acceptance is addressed in PTI DC10.2 and in ACI 318 Ch. 26; I have not verified the exact section numbers for the editions adopted on this job, so do not cite a section from this app in an RFI or a submittal. Confirm against (1) the PT shop drawing General Notes, (2) the project specification, and (3) the edition the AHJ has adopted. Where the shop drawing and the spec differ, the conflict is an RFI — not a field judgment call.
Lift-off band. The min/max % GUTS above brackets the effective force you expect to see after seating and short-term losses. If the shop drawing or spec does not state a lift-off acceptance band, that is a gap — RFI it rather than picking a number here and letting the record imply it was approved.

Tendon schedule read from the drawing

Candidate tendon marks

Read this before trusting it. This is pattern-matching on the drawing's text layer, not drawing interpretation. It collects strings shaped like a tendon mark — one to three letters followed by a number, such as T12, B4 or U-7. It cannot tell a tendon mark from a detail bubble, a rebar call-out, or a column line, and it will miss any mark that does not fit that shape. Check every one against the tendon schedule before you generate a sheet, and enter lengths and calculated elongations from the schedule yourself.
